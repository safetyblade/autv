> A home television guide for the curated AU TV lineup. The channel numbers and descriptions below are generated from the authoritative working guide.

## Quick start

### AU TV app — recommended

1. Install the current **AU TV v0.4.0** APK on the Android TV / Google TV device.
2. Open AU TV. The app loads the published channel guide and current playlist data used by the project.
3. Browse the guide by genre, search for a channel or programme, or enter a channel number directly.
4. Select a channel to tune. Use the in-player guide/chrome to move between channels without returning to the launcher.
5. On supported mobile Android devices, Picture-in-Picture remains available. TV operation is designed around the remote and full-screen guide.
6. After a lineup refresh, reopen/reload the guide so newly published channels and numbering are picked up.

### Legacy Live Channels setup

The project still publishes an M3U playlist and combined XMLTV EPG for compatible TV-input apps. Use this only where the native AU TV app is not being used. Import the published playlist, allow its EPG to load, then rescan/refresh after lineup changes. Google still documents Live Channels as a source-based TV app on compatible Android TV devices, although the AU TV native app is now the primary household path.

## Using this guide

Channel numbers are grouped by genre rather than assigned alphabetically. Inside each block, channels follow the project's editorial subgenre order. **Channel only** means the service is retained without a reliable programme schedule; **Full** means programme data is expected in the combined EPG.

# Visual direction

The guide should visually belong to the AU TV app rather than imitate a generic magazine template.

- **Background / ink:** near-black **#070A10**.
- **Primary accent / paper tone:** warm stone **#CEC4B4**.
- Use warm off-white pages or panels, near-black typography, and stone rules/section markers for printable layouts.
- Keep genre navigation structural rather than rainbow-coded. The app identity is restrained and premium; many bright category colours would break that identity.
- Channel numbers should be the strongest navigation element, followed by channel name, then description/EPG detail.
- Use generous spacing, strong section openers and compact channel entries. Avoid dense spreadsheet-style tables for the household reading version.
- For screen layouts, use the near-black field as the dominant surface and stone for focus, selection and key controls; reserve white for high-legibility programme text.

## Maintenance rule

The authoritative lineup remains `channel_guide.csv`. Update the CSV first, refresh the generated playlist/guide, then regenerate this household guide. Do not manually maintain a competing channel list here.
