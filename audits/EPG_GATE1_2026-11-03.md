**Owner:** Mara Collins, Channel Operations & Support  
**Review date:** 3 November 2026  
**Scope:** FAST/IPTV schedule completeness, programme mapping and Australian-provider EPG inputs; no numbering, player, auth or UI changes.

## Verified repository baseline

- `channel_guide.csv`: 636 active FAST/IPTV entries. Its `EPG` column is a desired capability class, **not measured coverage**.
- `playlist.m3u`: 636 unique numbered entries on the initial Gate 1 inspection. 
- `guide.json`: 636 active, 636 marked available. `available` means a configured stream URL exists; not real-time reachability or rights.
- FAST channel numbers currently begin at 100. The native Australian broadcaster catalogue is not represented in `channel_guide.csv`.
- `scripts/build_epg.py` merges programme data from Plex AU, Pluto US/GB/CA, Roku, Xumo, Tubi and Samsung. Exact `tvg-id` match is preferred; fallback maps by canonical name when only one candidate with programmes exists. It skips channels without an EPG mapping and adds Android genre categories.
- `native/tools/build_guide.py` creates guide.json from CSV and numbered playlist, not programme schedules.

## Measured programme quality — source of truth

The merged published `epg.xml.gz` is the measurement input, read by `scripts/audit_epg_gate1.py`; no inference from `EPG=Full`. Automated evidence files:
- `audits/epg_gate1_summary.json`: measured-at UTC time, count of published channels, channels with programme data, current and next programmes, forward 24h coverage (80% and 95%), gaps/overlaps, and misleading Full claims, plus genre breakdown.
- `audits/epg_gate1_channel_audit.csv`: every approved channel number, source, guide claim, actual XMLTV ID, programme count, current/next titles, forward 24h percentage, gaps, overlaps, invalid events, latest programme end and disposition.

**Measurement status:** Live compressed XMLTV cannot be read as a normal text file through the connected GitHub Contents view. The audit workflow runs directly against the repository's `epg.xml.gz` and saves outputs as GitHub Actions artifacts; numeric programme coverage must come from a successful run, not be invented. It is automatically dispatched by the audit PR merge and scheduled daily.

## Identified EPG reliability and matching risks

1. **Guide claim inflation.** `Full` does not imply a current programme is available. Displayed "No programme information" must be driven by actual schedule events.
2. **Source provenance lost.** The combined EPG does not currently persist which upstream region/file supplied each channel or when that source was acquired. Matching origin and freshness investigations are therefore limited without refresh logs.
3. **Exact-ID failures.** Replacement M3U Pluto channel IDs may differ by region; canonical-name fallback runs only when exactly one candidate with programme events exists. Ambiguous names and channels with no programmes may have no EPG.
4. **Schedule freshness and regional time.** XMLTV uses timestamp offsets; device comparisons must normalise to instants, then display in the configured local region. UTC audit avoids Melbourne AEDT/standard-time ambiguity.
5. **No guarantee that EPG equals live availability.** Programme listings, stream URL publication, live playback and provider authorisation are four separate states.
6. **Forward coverage and gaps.** A channel with one current programme but a large forward gap is not "full EPG". The 24h coverage metrics quantify this.
7. **Provider overlap.** Similarly named ABC News/FAST or broadcaster channels must not borrow schedules from an unrelated service without a stable crosswalk.

## Australian TV provider schedule-source inventory

The Android native data model has `ProviderChannelId(provider, channel)`, `ProviderChannel(..., programmes)` and `ProviderProgramme(title,start,end)`. `ProviderAdapter.channels()` supplies catalogue and schedules. The app's provider layer owns the account/session state; the implementation notes say native schedules reach `EpgRepository` independently of FAST.

| Service | Native route | Programme source | Gate 1 evidence / caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9Now | Native Nine adapter | Provider catalogue programme list | Owner previously reported playback success; measure actual next-24h schedule on device |
| 7plus | Native Seven adapter | Provider live catalogue programme list | Owner reported playback success; do not change Seven adapter; actual EPG coverage not measured here |
| SBS | Native SBS adapter | Provider catalogue programme list | Owner previously reported playback success; actual EPG coverage not measured here |
| ABC iview | Native ABC adapter | Provider catalogue programme list | Connection/media authorisation dependent on approved configuration; programme completeness unverified |
| Network 10 | Native Ten adapter | Provider catalogue programme list | Client registration and live media authorisation dependent; programme completeness unverified |

The FAST XMLTV audit **does not measure** these five native providers. No fixed numbers should be assigned until Helena signs off an explicit regional channel inventory and a collision-free AUTV numbering map. Do not confuse `ABC News Live` FAST #104 with an authenticated ABC iview channel.
